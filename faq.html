Frequently asked Questions

February weather in Copan?

February in Copán is usually warm during the day and cooler in the evenings. We recommend packing light, breathable clothing for the daytime and a light jacket or wrap for nighttime events. Comfortable shoes are also recommended if you plan to walk around town or visit the ruins.

Are children invited to the wedding?

We love your little ones, but this celebration will be an adults-only event. We appreciate your understanding and can't wait to celebrate with you!

What should I wear to each event?

Please refer to our Attire Page for dress code details and inspiration for each event of the wedding weekend. View Attire Page.

How do I get from San Pedro Sula to Copán Ruinas?

Guests may coordinate their preferred transportation to Copán Ruinas. DMundo Travel can assist with private or group transfers, while rental cars are also available for those who prefer to drive.

How can I move inside Copan Ruinas?

Getting around Copán Ruinas is easy. For local transportation, please coordinate directly with the hotel front desk, and they can help arrange it for you.

Will transportation be provided from San Pedro Sula to Copán Ruinas?

Yes. A group shuttle from San Pedro Sula to Copán Ruinas will be available at a scheduled departure time. Guests who are unable to make the scheduled departure, or who would prefer private transportation, may coordinate directly with DMundo Travel.
